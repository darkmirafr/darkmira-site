    Our audits in detail

Security is considered at every phase of an application's life.

Our reference is OWASP. Its recommendations guide us from design to maintenance, not just at delivery time.

  1. Design

    Sensitive data and risks are identified from the very first architecture decisions.

  2. Development

    Code follows OWASP recommendations, and every change is reviewed before it is merged.

  3. Release

    Automated tests run on every change. Nothing goes into production without passing them.

  4. Maintenance

    Dependencies are monitored, and known vulnerabilities are fixed as soon as they are disclosed.

Our developer charter.

Every Darkmira developer signs it. It describes how we work, and it comes down to three ideas.

The entrepreneurial spirit

A Darkmira developer does more than carry out tasks. They commit to their projects and to their own growth.

Commitment
They are proactive, autonomous and take responsibility.
Initiative
They readily share what they know with the team and with their network.
Mutual support
Their colleagues know they can count on them.

The hacker spirit

In the original sense of the word: curiosity, and the drive to understand how things work.

Involvement
They keep up with technical news, contribute to open source and take part in events.
Sharing
They pass on what they know and discuss good practices.
Experimentation
They try things out to understand them, rather than putting up with the technology.

The engineering spirit

They write applications built to last.

Reliability
They check that the application does what is required, with automated tests.
Maintainability
They follow good practices and automate quality control.
Security
They think about security at every stage of an application's life.
